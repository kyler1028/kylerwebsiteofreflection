Workshop: Data Visualization

This week's workshop focused on data visualisation and how to present data in a clear and meaningful way. We first reviewed the data collection task from the previous week and discussed some of the challenges we experienced when generating and organising datasets.

Based on last workshop, our group worked on collecting AI policies from five UK universities. However, we found it difficult to organise and code the information because many university guidelines were quite general and lacked specific details. As a result, the amount of data we could collect was limited, making it difficult to create meaningful visualisations.

We realised that collecting more detailed information would require us to investigate the policies of different schools, departments and individual courses. We might also need to interview teachers and students to understand how AI rules are actually applied in practice. However, this would involve a significant amount of time and money, which was difficult for us to manage.

After discussing the experience of data collection and analysis, we then learned about different types of data, variables and charts, such as bar charts, pie charts and line charts. The workshop introduced tools like Excel and Tableau Public, which can help us analyse and visualise data.
